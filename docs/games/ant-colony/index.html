HOW TO PLAY

Trails, brood and war
🐜
Trails do the work. Ants searching for food leave a blue home trail behind them. Ants carrying food home leave a green food trail. Other ants smell those trails and follow them, so a lucky find quickly becomes a highway.
🍯
Food is everything. Every ant eats from the store each day. Run dry and ants starve and the queen weakens. Seeds and sugar run out; aphid herds slowly regrow.
🥚
Set caste targets on the left. The queen automatically lays eggs toward those numbers while food allows and a brood slot is free.
⛏
Spend food on upgrades (right panel): tunnels for storage and brood slots, antennae for scent range, mandibles for bigger loads, and the scout and soldier castes.
👑
Protect the queen. Rival raiders and beetles that reach your nest wound her. She dies, the colony dies.
⚔
Rally soldiers with R at the cursor — park them on a trail choke point, or on the rival nest to tear it down.
🌧
Weather matters. Rain washes trails away and slows the colony. Cold snaps raise upkeep. Check the forecast each dawn.
🏁
Objective: keep the queen alive for 20 days. Score rewards food gathered, colony size, upgrades and razing the rival nest.
