LIVING SANDBOX

Gaia

Seed a world with plants, prey and predators. Every creature carries a heritable genome — speed, senses and metabolism mutate each generation — and the food web has to survive shifting seasons.

Field Guide

Pick a tool, then click or drag anywhere on the field to paint life into the world. Prey graze on plants and flee predators; predators hunt prey. Every birth mutates the parent's genome, so predation pressure really does breed faster prey.
