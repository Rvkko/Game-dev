About & Disclaimer

About this prototype

The Pilot Fuel Tanker Training Prototype is an interactive, browser-based 3D simulator that familiarizes users with the components, layout, and systems of a gasoline tanker rig — from the tractor cab to the trailer’s cargo compartments. It is built to help people recognize and understand the equipment, not to operate it.

Intended use

  • Recognize and name the major components of a tractor and gasoline tanker trailer.
  • Understand what each part is, why it matters, and what to look at during a visual review.
  • Build conceptual awareness of the cab, engine bay, coupling, and tanker systems.

What it is not

This prototype does not train anyone to operate a tanker, couple a trailer, or perform any fuel transfer. All content is framed as awareness of equipment and systems, never as authoritative operating instruction.

Review status

Before any real-world use, this material requires sign-off from subject-matter experts, safety, legal, hazmat trainers, and compliance. Track that in the SME Review panel. Full documentation is in TRAINING_PRODUCT_README.md and TRAINING_QA_CHECKLIST.md.

Help & Controls

How to use the simulator

Navigating the 3D model

  • Orbit: drag with the mouse or one finger.
  • Zoom: scroll wheel, or pinch on touch.
  • Pan: right-drag (desktop) or two-finger drag.
  • Inspect a part: tap or click it to open its info panel. In a module, this checks it off your task list.
  • All controls: open the ☰ Controls menu inside the viewer for lights, doors, x-ray, fluids, modes, and more.

Keyboard (inside the viewer)

W A S D / arrows drive or walk · E engine · L lights · H horn.

Pause & restart

Press Esc or P anywhere to pause. The pause panel can resume, restart the current module, assessment, or tour, or send you back to the dashboard.

Training flow

  • Work the modules in order — each launches the 3D model in the right view and gives you a task and a checkpoint question.
  • Take the assessment when you’re ready. Retake it any time.
  • Your progress saves automatically in this browser.

Accessibility

  • Full keyboard navigation and visible focus outlines throughout the shell.
  • Every part and module has descriptive text — nothing is conveyed by the 3D visuals alone.
  • Module task steps can be completed with the on-screen checklist buttons if 3D clicking isn’t available to you.
  • Honors your system “reduce motion” setting.
