HOW TO PLAY

Move the mouse target over the strike zone and click as the pitch crosses the plate. Arrow keys or WASD aim and Space swings.

Timing controls pull/opposite-field direction; aim and pitch location control launch angle. Misses and non-homers count as outs. Hit 6 home runs before 10 outs to win. Esc or P pauses.
