HOW TO PLAY

Move with W/S or the mouse. The paddle returns on contact. Press Space or click just before contact for a smash. A adds topspin; D adds backspin.

Spin curves after the center-line bounce. First to 7, win by two. Beat four increasingly sharp opponents to become champion. Esc or P pauses.
