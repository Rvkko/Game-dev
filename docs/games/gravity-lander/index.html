HOW TO PLAY
Guide your lander down onto the glowing green pad. Thrust with W, rotate with A/D. Rotating tilts your engine so thrust pushes you sideways — non-inverted, tilt left to drift left. Hold SHIFT for a fuel-hungry thrust boost. Orbit the camera by dragging the mouse or with Q/E, scroll to zoom. Watch your descent speed and tilt — land too fast or too tilted and you'll crash. Manage fuel: run dry and you're at the mercy of gravity and wind. Softer, flatter, faster-fuel-remaining landings earn better medals.
