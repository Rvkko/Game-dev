COMMAND

Lead a carrier group through five real-time fleet operations. Capture relays, escort civilians, and break the siege.

TACTICAL MANUAL

Select: click/tap a friendly ship, or drag a selection box. Order: right-click or tap empty space to move. Select Focus Fire then tap an enemy.

Ships auto-fire in range. Capture blue relays for supplies. Repair Drone costs 30 supply. Destroy the enemy flagship or complete the mission objective.

Keyboard: 1 Move, 2 Focus, 3 Repair, Esc / P / Space to pause, R to restart the mission.
