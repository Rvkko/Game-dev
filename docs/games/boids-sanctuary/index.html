HOW TO PLAY
Shepherd, don't command
Boids have no leader. Each one only obeys three rules — separation, alignment and cohesion — applied to the neighbours it can see. The flock's whole personality lives in those three sliders, and you can move them mid-flight from the TUNING drawer. That is the real control scheme.

Winning

Each challenge shows its objective top-left: hold N boids inside a green sanctuary ring, survive a hawk hunt, or feed the flock. Watch the timer and the LOST counter — hawks eat stragglers and stone kills nobody but wastes precious seconds. Faster clears with bigger surviving flocks earn Silver and Gold.

Pro tips

· Lower Cohesion to split a flock in two; raise it to squeeze through a gap.
· Raise Alignment for long straight migrations, drop it for tight turns.
· High Separation makes the flock spread wide — great against hawks, terrible in canyons.
· Seeds outrank everything: drop one inside the sanctuary and the flock parks itself.
· A pulse fired inside a canyon will splatter your flock into the walls. Use it on hawks.
