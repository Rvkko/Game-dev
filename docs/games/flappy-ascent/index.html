HOW TO PLAY

ONE BUTTON, INFINITE SKY

CONTROLS

SPACEFlap — one press, one wingbeat. Also starts a run. W ↑Flap (same as Space). CLICKFlap. Tap anywhere on the sky — touch works too. ESC PPause / resume. Esc backs out of any menu. RRestart the run instantly. MMute / unmute the sound.

THE CLIMB

  • Fly through the gap in every gate. Touch a pylon or the ground and the run ends.
  • Dead-centre passes score a PERFECT: +50 and a rising chime. Chain them for the Threadneedle award.
  • Every 10 gates you break into a new sky zone — faster, tighter, prettier.
  • Drifting gates sway, narrow gold gates are worth double, and shutter gates breathe open and shut. Time them.
  • Grab 🪶 feathers for +75 and a small burst of lift.
  • Your score multiplier climbs to x5 the deeper you go — every gate is worth more than the last.

GHOST & MEDALS

  • A translucent ghost flies your personal best beside you, and a glowing beacon marks exactly where that run ended. Pass it for a NEW RECORD.
  • Medals are awarded on gates cleared: 🥉 Bronze 10 · 🥈 Silver 25 · 🥇 Gold 45 (Storm needs 8 / 20 / 36).
  • Breezy is practice: wider gaps, slower sky, no medals or awards.
  • The Daily Ascent is the same 80-gate course for everyone today. Clear all 80 to reach the summit.
