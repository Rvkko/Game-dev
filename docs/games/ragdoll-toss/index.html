PHYSICS ARCADE

Ragdoll Toss

Three throws per run. Charge the cannon, pick your angle, then steer the crash dummy through coins and bounce pads all the way to the Moon Gate at 1,000 metres.

Cannon Upgrades

Coins you collect on the course are permanent. Spend them here — you will need the extra muscle to clear 1,000 metres.

BANK: 0 COINS

How to Play

W / S
Raise or lower the cannon angle (while aiming)
Space
Hold to charge, release to launch
Click / Hold
Same as Space — charge and release on the course
W A S D
Steer in flight (burns air control fuel)
Shift
Air boost — a hard kick forward and up
Esc
Pause / resume
P
Pause / resume
R
Restart the run

Land on an orange bounce pad and you get launched again instead of skidding to a stop. Every throw ends when the dummy stops moving; a run is three throws. Reach 1,000 metres in a single throw to clear the Moon Gate.
