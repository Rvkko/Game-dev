HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD or Arrow keys. Move the mouse across the opponent's court to choose shot direction. Press Space or click to serve and swing.

Time your swing when the ball is close. Early shots go cross-court; late shots go down the line. Each side gets one bounce. Standard tennis points and deuce apply; first to three games wins. Esc pauses.
