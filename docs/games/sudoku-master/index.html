The rules

Every row, every column and every 3×3 box must contain the digits 1 through 9 exactly once. The grey clue digits are fixed. Every generated puzzle is guaranteed to have exactly one solution, so you never need to guess — there is always a logical next step.

Input

Cell first: click (or arrow to) a cell, then press 1–9 or tap the number pad.
Number first: tap a pad number to arm it (it glows), then click cells to stamp it in. Space stamps the armed digit into the selected cell.
Pencil notes: press N to toggle notes mode, or hold Shift while typing a digit. Q fills candidate notes on every empty cell.

Tools

Hint solves one cell for you and names the technique that proves it — a real lesson, not a reveal (limited per puzzle).
Check highlights every wrong entry on the board, at a cost of 30 seconds on the clock.
Undo/Redo walk the whole move history, including auto-notes and restarts.

Scoring

Score scales with difficulty and how far under par you finish, minus penalties for mistakes, hints and checks. Solve puzzles back to back to build a win streak — a mistake-limit loss or quitting mid-puzzle does not break it, but losing does reset it. In strict mode (default) a wrong digit is flagged instantly and three errors end the run.
