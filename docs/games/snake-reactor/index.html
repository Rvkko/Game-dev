HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Steer
WASD or ↑←↓→
Inputs are buffered — you can queue two turns and they fire on the next two ticks.

Boost Space — burn forward at double tick rate while held. Nothing to charge, just nerve.

Pause Esc / P  ·  Restart R

Pointer — swipe or drag anywhere on the core to steer. A touch D-pad can be switched on in Settings.
Score
Each fuel cell is worth 10 × level × your combo multiplier. Eat again before the combo bar drains and the multiplier climbs to x5. Let it lapse and you drop back to x1.

Speed curve
Every 6 cells raises the reactor level and shortens the tick. Level 15 is roughly twice the starting speed.

Sectors
In Maze and Portal, every 8 cells triggers a Reactor Shift: the layout swaps, your snake is re-seated safely, and you bank a sector bonus.

Power-ups

  • SLOW-MO — stretches the tick by 75% for 7 seconds. Best used to untangle a long coil.
  • SHRINK — vents a third of your tail instantly and pays out points for what you burn.
  • MAGNET — the fuel cell walks toward your head for 9 seconds and snaps in from one tile away.
  • SURGE — rare gold cell: 4× points and +2 combo, but it decays fast.

Death

You die by hitting a bulkhead, the outer wall (Classic and Maze only — Portal wraps), or your own body. Turning into the tile your tail is about to vacate is legal, so tight coils are survivable if your timing is exact.
