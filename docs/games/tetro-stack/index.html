HOW TO PLAY
STACK · CLEAR · SURVIVE
Pieces fall from the top of the well. Fill a complete horizontal row and it clears, awarding points and keeping your stack alive. Top out — a new piece has nowhere to spawn — and the run ends.

SCORING

Single 100 · Double 300 · Triple 500 · Quad 800 — all multiplied by level.
T-Spin: rotate a T-piece into a slot where 3 of its 4 corners are blocked. T-Spin Single 800, Double 1200, Triple 1600.
Back-to-Back: chain Quads and T-Spins with no plain clears between them for a 1.5× bonus.
Combo: clear lines with consecutive pieces — 50 × combo × level each time.
Perfect Clear: empty the whole board for a huge bonus.

SYSTEMS

The 7-bag randomiser deals every piece once before repeating, so droughts never last. SRS wall kicks let pieces twist into tight gaps against walls and overhangs. The faded ghost shows where the piece lands. Lock delay gives you half a second of sliding before a grounded piece sets — but only 15 resets, so don't stall forever.

MODES

Marathon — 150 lines. Gravity rises every 10 lines up to level 20. Clear it to win.
Sprint 40L — clear 40 lines against the clock at your chosen (fixed) level.
Ultra 2:00 — score as much as possible in two minutes; levels still rise.
