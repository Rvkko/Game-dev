HOW TO PLAY

Move with A/D or Left/Right. W/Up jumps. Space bumps or spikes toward the mouse target. E switches between teammates. Click the court to hit.

When the HUD reads SERVE, press Space or click the court to put the ball in play. Your partner positions automatically. Each team gets three touches. Win two sets; each set is first to 7, win by two. Esc or P pauses.
