HOW TO PLAY

Race down a procedural neon tunnel. Distance is your score. Obstacles need the right move — dodge sideways, jump low barriers, slide under overhead bars, or blast destructible crates. Collect coins for the garage, and energy orbs to fuel Overdrive. Speed ramps the further you go.
