SCORING

Every brick you break without touching the paddle raises the combo multiplier — up to x3.4. Touching the paddle or losing a ball resets it. Clearing fast, keeping balls alive and chaining explosions is how you beat par and take three stars. Bosses appear on levels 10, 20 and 30: dodge their bombs, keep hitting the core, and clear the surrounding bricks to finish the stage.
