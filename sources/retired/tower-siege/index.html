HOW TO PLAY

Attackers march the lit road from the west gate to the east gate. Every leak costs a life — lose all 20 and the fortress falls. Survive all ten waves to win.

  • Build — pick a tower on the bottom bar, then click open ground away from the road.
  • Upgrade — click one of your towers to open its panel: upgrade to level 3 or sell it for 60% back.
  • Earn — gold comes from kills and a bonus for every wave cleared.

Towers: Bolt is cheap and fast · Frost chills attackers · Cannon deals splash damage · Tesla arcs to three foes · Sniper reaches across the map.

Controls

  • Mouse — click to build or to select a tower.
  • WASD / Arrow keys — move the build cursor.
  • Space — build at the cursor (or start the wave when no tower type is selected).
  • 1–5 — pick tower type · Q deselect · U upgrade · X sell.
  • Enter / N — start the next wave · F — toggle 1x/2x speed.
  • Esc or P — pause · R — restart the campaign.
