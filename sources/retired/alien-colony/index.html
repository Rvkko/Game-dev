COLONY SURVIVAL

Xeno Frontier

Your pod is down on a hostile world. Mine ore, grow food, keep the oxygen flowing and the lights on, then research the escape blueprint and get your survivors home before the xenos overrun you.

How to Play

Pick a structure from the bottom bar, then click the ground to queue it. Colonists walk over and build it, then staff it automatically. Everything costs materials, and most things cost power.

Survive: keep oxygen generation above consumption, food above zero, and at least one colonist alive. Win: research Escape Blueprint, build the Escape Ship, and launch with three or more survivors.
